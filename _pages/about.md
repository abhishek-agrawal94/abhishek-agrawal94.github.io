I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Center for Data Science in Humanities at Chosun University, South Korea. My current work involves using computational models to analyze long-form audio recordings and ego-centric video recordings to understand how children acquire language by interacting with their environment.

I completed my Ph.D. in 2025 from Aix-Marseille Université, France under the supervision of [Abdellah Fourtassi](https://afourtassi.github.io/) and [Benoit Favre](https://pageperso.lis-lab.fr/benoit.favre/). My background is in computational linguistics (Master's degree at Charles University, Prague) and computer science (Bachelor's degree from the University of Pune).

I am primarily interested in studying the development of children's socio-cognitive abilities and their language acquisition. In particular, I am interested in understanding how children learn from social interaction by studying large scale longitudinal data. For this purpose, I make use of machine learning models from Natural Language Processing (NLP) as proxies for analysing the behaviour of children having social interactions on a large scale.
